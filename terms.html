The service

At an alarm time you set, the service texts you a short arithmetic question. A correct reply confirms you are awake, and one follow-up check is sent a few minutes later. If you do not respond, the service may text one trusted contact, but only if that contact first agreed by replying YES to a consent message.

Messages

Message frequency depends on the alarms you set, typically a few messages per alarm to the user. A trusted contact receives one consent message and, only after replying YES, at most one alert text per alarm. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
