Opt-in Workflow - Wake Check

Wake Check is a prototype wake-up verification app. This page documents how recipients consent before receiving texts. Contact: utilitechfan@gmail.com. See the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.

1. The user (alarm owner)

The user enters their own mobile number in the app and sets an alarm. They only receive wake-up questions for alarms they created. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

2. The trusted contact (optional, double opt-in by text)

If the user adds a trusted contact's number, the only message sent to that number is this welcome and consent request, sent from +18886323490:

Sam added you as a wake-up accountability contact for "get up for school", alarm Tue Oct 13 at 06:30 AM CDT. If they don't respond to wake-up texts, you'll get one alert text per alarm. Reply YES to agree or NO to decline. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Terms: https://aayansharma.github.io/wakeup-legal/terms.html Privacy: https://aayansharma.github.io/wakeup-legal/privacy.html

Opt-in keyword: the contact replies YES from their own number. The service accepts the reply only from the number the consent message was sent to.

Message frequency: a contact receives the consent message and, only after replying YES, at most one alert text per alarm.

3. Confirmation message (sent after YES)

You're all set! You'll only get a text if they don't wake up. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply NO, or no reply: the contact receives no further messages.
  • Reply STOP at any time: all texts to that number stop.
